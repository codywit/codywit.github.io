Hello, I am Cody Witmer. I am currently a freshman at Millersville University. I am a Technology and Engineering Education Major and hope to become a tech ed teacher after getting my degree here. I am currently not involved in any clubs but plan on possibly joining the club lacrosse team next year. While writing this I am currently enrolled in AENG 110 Communication and Information Systems, which is why I am making this website, and I assume why you are reading it. In this class we learned a whole variety of skills related to communication and information systems. We learned to use InDesign, to sketch with perspective, take videos and edit them into movies, and to take photos and edit them in photoshop.

While that is not the extent of what we learned in this class, those are some of the highlights and my personal favorites. With that in mind, the whole reason I made this website was to display some of my work I made in this class. Some of the things displayed are my digital photography assignment, my infographic, and my groups video about Millersville athletic facilities.
