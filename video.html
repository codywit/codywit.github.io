Student at Millersville University

This is me and my groups video project. We were tasked with filming, producing, and editing a video somewhere on Millersvilles campus. My group decided to give a tour of all the athletic facilities on campus.

We started off this project by storyboarding our different scenes. Then after that we made a script and went out to record our scenes. Once we had all the clips we needed we went back to the classroom to edit all the clips togeher as well as adding effects and text.
